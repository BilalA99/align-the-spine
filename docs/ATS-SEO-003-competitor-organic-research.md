## Strategic takeaways (patterns, not copied claims)

These are structural/architectural observations for later content planning — none of them are
claims about Align, and none should be read as "copy this wording":

1. **The bar is low.** All 3 competitors combined have 48 organic keywords and under $530/month
   in combined traffic value. This is a market with almost no organic competition to beat, not an
   entrenched field.
2. **County Line's page architecture is worth studying (not copying):** `/accident-type/[type]`,
   `/locations/[city]` + `/es/` Spanish mirrors, `/treatments/[treatment]`,
   `/doctors/[name]`. Align already has a comparable shape (`/conditions/*`, `/service-areas/*`,
   `/services/*`) — the gap isn't architecture, it's that most of Align's equivalent pages are
   still `draft` (see ATS-SEO-040/042 work already shipped this session).
3. **Spanish-language "near me" queries are a real, currently-uncaptured cluster.** County Line's
   single richest page (11 keyword variants) is entirely Spanish-language local-intent queries.
   Align has no Spanish content today. This is a content-gap _opportunity flag_ for whoever scopes
   Epic F, not a recommendation to act on unilaterally here — Align's own bilingual-care claim
   (`content/doctor-profile.ts`) already establishes Dr. Abe is bilingual, so it's at least
   plausible groundwork exists.
4. **`/conditions/back-pain`'s eventual publication has direct evidence behind it:** Bartell's
   `/back-pain-2/` page proves "back pain deerfield" (vol 60) is a real, winnable query even a
   weak competitor can rank position 10 for.
5. **Neck pain, sciatica, and whiplash are wide open.** None of the 3 competitors rank for any of
   these — Align isn't playing catch-up on these queries, it's uncontested (once those pages
   publish).
6. **AI Overview is already displacing some of these competitors' traffic**, particularly Carpe
   Diem's — several of their lost keywords now show AI Overview in the SERP. Worth keeping in mind
   for how future content is structured (direct-answer framing), not actionable within this
   ticket.
